Account Deletion Request

Users of Granite Marble Measure Sheet can request deletion of their account and associated data at any time.

How to Request Account Deletion

Please send an email to:

helpgmmeasure@gmail.com

Include your registered email address in the request.

What Data Will Be Deleted

  • User account information (name, email)
  • Measurement sheets and entries
  • Uploaded slab images
  • Business profile details

Deletion Timeline

All account data will be permanently deleted within 7 working days of receiving the request.

Some information may be retained if required by law or for security purposes.
